## App name (max 30 chars)
Scam or Safe?

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Beat the clock, spot 45 real scams, and see if you're a cyber sentinel.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Can you tell a scam from the real thing before the clock runs out?

Scam or Safe? is a fast-paced cybersecurity quiz built around 45 real-life
scenarios — texts, calls, emails, and everyday moments online, including a
set focused on the scams teens run into most. Read each one and decide fast:
is it safe, or a scam?

HOW IT PLAY
• Read the scenario.
• Tap "Looks safe" or "It's a scam" before time runs out.
• Get it right and the disco lights come on. Get it wrong and you're doing
  time behind bars.
• After all 45 scenarios, see your score and find out if you're a Cyber
  Sentinel or if you need to slow down and look twice next time.

WHY IT MATTERS
Scammers count on quick, distracted taps. Every scenario comes with a short
tip explaining exactly what gave the scam away — or what made the safe
moment safe — so you walk away sharper than you started, whether it's gift
card scams, fake tech support calls, phishing texts, or fraudulent QR codes.

• 45 real-world scenarios covering phishing, phone scams, gift-card fraud,
  social engineering, teen-specific scams (gaming, social media, part-time
  jobs), and everyday good security habits
• A ticking clock that keeps you on your toes
• Instant feedback with a short "why" on every answer
• No ads, no accounts, no data collection — just play

Whether you're brushing up on your own instincts or helping a family member
get sharper about scams, Scam or Safe? turns cybersecurity training into a
quick, replayable game.
