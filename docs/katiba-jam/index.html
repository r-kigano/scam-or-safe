Katiba Jam — Support

A colourful, accessible way to learn the Constitution of Kenya, 2010, for every age.

Get help

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email the developer, Rachel Kigano, at rkigano@gmail.com. Please mention your device (for example, iPhone 15 or Samsung Galaxy A15) if something isn't working.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There's no sign-in, and the app works straight away.
  • The text is too small or hard to read. Open the Control Booth in the app to make text bigger, turn on High Contrast, or switch sound off.
  • Read-aloud is silent. Check that your device isn't muted and that sound is on in the Control Booth. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice.
  • Can I read the full Constitution? Yes. "Read the Whole Katiba" has all 18 chapters, and you can search by word or article number.
  • Does the app collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.

About the content

Katiba Jam is an independent educational app. It is not a government app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Kenya or any of its agencies.

The constitutional text is the Constitution of Kenya, 2010, as officially published by:

  • Kenya Law (National Council for Law Reporting): new.kenyalaw.org
  • Parliament of Kenya: The Constitution of Kenya, 2010 (PDF)
