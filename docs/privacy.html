Privacy Policy

Scam or Safe?

Effective and last updated August 13, 2026

Scam or Safe? is a short cybersecurity training quiz. This policy is short too, because the app doesn't collect anything.

Scam or Safe? does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no backend server of its own. Your score, timer, and progress through the quiz exist only in the app's memory while you're playing — nothing is written to a file, a cookie, or local storage, and none of it is sent anywhere. Closing the app clears it completely.

Third-party services

The app loads one external resource: its display typeface, served from Google Fonts. Fetching that file exposes your device's IP address and basic request metadata to Google, the same way loading any web font or image from a remote server does. Google's handling of that request is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. No other network requests are made by the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. Scam or Safe? is safe for players of any age who are old enough to read the scenarios.

Permissions

On Android, the app requests only the INTERNET permission, used exclusively to load the typeface mentioned above. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect data — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
