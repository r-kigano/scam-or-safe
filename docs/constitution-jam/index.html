Constitution Jam — Support

A musical, colorful way to learn the U.S. Constitution, for all ages.

Get help

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email the developer, Rachel Kigano, at rkigano@gmail.com. Please mention your device (for example, iPhone 15 or Pixel 8) if something isn't working.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There's no sign-in, and the app works straight away.
  • The text is too small or hard to read. Open the Control Booth in the app to make text bigger, turn on High Contrast, or switch sound off.
  • Read-aloud is silent. Check that your device isn't muted and that sound is on in the Control Booth. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice.
  • Does the app collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.

About the content

Constitution Jam is an independent educational app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any government entity. The text of the Constitution comes from the National Archives' official transcript: archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript.
