Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no accounts, no ads, and no data collected.

Why does the clock stop after I answer?

The clock pauses while you read the tip. The game moves on after 15 seconds, or sooner if you tap Continue.

Can I see my answers again?

Yes. When a run ends, tap "Review answers" to see every scenario, what you answered, the correct answer, and the explanation.

I think a scenario is wrong or unclear.

Please email the scenario's wording and what you would change, and I'll take a look.
